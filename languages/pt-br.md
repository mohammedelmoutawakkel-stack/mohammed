# Portuguese (Brazil) — Language Notes

Use alongside `SKILL.md`, referenced from its Voice Calibration section. The English "words to watch" lists in `SKILL.md` are illustrations of an underlying rhetorical move, not a literal vocabulary to translate — the moves show up in Brazilian Portuguese as different stock phrases entirely. This file lists the actual PT-BR phrasing and register conventions to watch for.

## AI-flavored vocabulary and phrasing

Parallels `SKILL.md` §1 (significance inflation), §5 (vague attributions), §7 (AI vocabulary), §21 (cutoff/gap-filling), §27 (persuasive tropes).

**Phrases to watch:** é importante ressaltar que, é fundamental destacar, desempenha um papel fundamental/crucial, dessa forma, nesse sentido, além disso, ao longo do tempo, sem dúvida, cabe destacar, evidencia a importância de, consolidando-se como, no cenário atual, de forma a garantir

**Problem:** Same padding and inflation moves as the English list, expressed in PT-BR stock phrasing that a literal English-word search would miss entirely.

**Before:**
> É importante ressaltar que a empresa desempenha um papel fundamental no mercado, consolidando-se, dessa forma, como uma referência no setor.

**After:**
> A empresa é uma referência no setor.

## Register: shorthand vs. spelled-out formality

Parallels `SKILL.md` §23's new casual-register rows.

**Problem:** In casual PT-BR chat or Slack messages, people abbreviate constantly. AI defaults to fully spelled-out, formal phrasing even when the context calls for a quick internal message.

**Common shorthand AI tends to skip:**
- vlw (valeu) — thanks
- obg / obgda / obgdo (obrigado/a) — thanks
- blz (beleza) — cool / sounds good
- vc / cê (você) — you
- tá (está) — is/it's
- pq (porque / por que) — because / why
- qlqr (qualquer) — any
- tb / tbm (também) — also
- msg (mensagem) — message

**Before (quick internal message):**
> Muito obrigado pela ajuda! Qualquer coisa me avise.

**After:**
> Vlw pela ajuda! Qlqr coisa me chama.

This is descriptive of common register, not prescriptive. A user-provided writing sample (see `SKILL.md`'s Voice Calibration) still outranks this file — match the actual sample's mix of formal and shorthand instead of maximizing abbreviation.
